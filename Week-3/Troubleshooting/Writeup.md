Device: MacBook Pro 13-inch (2020)
Operating System: macOS Sequoia
1. Problem
My MacBook started overheating while I had approximately 15 browser tabs open. Even though the battery was at around 30%, the MacBook unexpectedly shut down.
2. Symptoms
The MacBook became unusually hot.
Multiple browser tabs were open.
The computer shut down unexpectedly.
The battery showed approximately 30% before shutdown.
3. Possible Causes
High CPU or memory usage from multiple open tabs.
Excessive heat buildup.
A possible battery or power-management issue.
The exact cause has not yet been confirmed.
4. Troubleshooting Steps
Allow the MacBook to cool down.
Close unnecessary browser tabs and applications.
Check Activity Monitor for applications using high CPU or memory.
Check the battery condition in System Settings.
Restart the MacBook and monitor its temperature and performance.
5. Conclusion
This incident showed how overheating and unexpected shutdowns can affect computer performance. Further monitoring is needed to determine whether the issue is related to workload, cooling, or battery condition.
